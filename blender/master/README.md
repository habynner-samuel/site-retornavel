# Coca-Cola Retornável (Sabor Original): modelo mestre v2

This is a reviewable version and not a manufacturer replica. Everything below is classified as **observed**, **estimated** or **pending**.

## Environment
- Blender 5.2 (detected in `C:\Program Files\Blender Foundation\Blender 5.2`), Cycles with OptiX on a GPU MX570.
- Python scripts are reproducible and have no manual steps.

| `master.py` | Builds everything. Run `blender -b -P master.py -- <output> <mode> [frames]`. Modes: `build`, `clay`, `vtest`, `diag`, `turntable`, `fill`, `still`. |

The diagnostic renders (clay, wireframe, overlay, light and dark backgrounds) are **not** promotional images. Only the `seq_turn` and `seq_fill` sequences go to the site.

## Diagnosis of the previous model (v1)
- **Silhouette:**
  - The shoulder was short and rounded. In the photo it is long and tapers all the way to the neck.
  - The body was straight. It had no waist below the label (about 9% narrower in the photo) and no bulge near the base.
  - The base was "domed" and had no visible curved heel.
- **Label:**
  - The art was invented: "RETORNÁVEL" in yellow at the top, a wavy ribbon, a round "RET" badge and a fake QR on the back.
  - The logo was set in a cursive font that imitates the original. The real logo was not used.
  - It carried text that is not on the real label ("2L", site address).
- **Scale:** fixed in "mm" with no measurement, presented as if it were real.
- **Materials:** 1,700 droplets covering the bottle and logo, with 9.5 min/frame in the first version. The cap was a lime green unrelated to the photo.
- **Camera:** not aligned to any reference, so there was no way to compare against the photo.

## What v2 does
- **Separate objects:** `BODY_PET`, `CAP_GREEN`, `TAMPER_RING`, `LABEL_ORIGINAL` and `LIQUID`, all parented to the empty `COCA_RETORNAVEL`.
- **Shape:**
  - Built as an editable radius-by-height profile (`PROFILE_PX`) in **pixels of photo 02**, measured column by column (axis x = 270.5).
  - Smoothed with Catmull-Rom, with corners only at the neck rings and at the label boundary.
  - The label section (y 176–301) is a straight cylinder, so the label does not distort.
- **Body:** a wall with an outer and an inner surface (Solidify), so it is not a surface with no thickness.
- **Liquid:** a closed volume inside the wall (0.08 gap, no overlap) with a subtle meniscus. The empty bottle uses the same body without `LIQUID`.
- **Label:** a thin film (0.06) over the PET. Its seam is at the back and the front sits at the centre of the UV. It uses its own material (glossy plastic film).
- **Lights:** these are **not visible through the PET** (`visible_transmission = False`). Otherwise the empty bottle turns into a white block.
- **Front overlay:** `renders/diag_sobreposicao_frente.png` (blue outline over the photo). The shoulder, label, waist, bulge and heel line up within about 1–2 px of the photo. Two caveats:
  - Photo 02 is in perspective and is not an orthographic view.
  - The scale comes from the body width.

## Label (provisional, front only)
- **Logo:** extracted from the real photo 02. It uses a white mask, unwrapped from the cylinder with u = R·asin(x/R), then smoothed by thresholding. The Coca-Cola mark was not redrawn and was not replaced with a font.
- **Text:** "SABOR ORIGINAL" and the "RETORNAVEL" band were recomposed in the photo's position and proportion with TCCC Unity Bold. In the photo those letters are about 6 px tall.
- **Accent:** photos 01 and 02 show **no visible accent** on "RETORNAVEL", so the art follows the photo. If the real label has the accent, change `spaced('RETORNAVEL', …)` in `make_label_original.py`.
- **Back:** plain red. There is no photo of the back, so nothing was invented: no nutrition table, barcode, badges, "2 L" or QR.
- **Resolution:** the logo comes from about 135 px in the photo, enlarged 8×. The outline is smoothed, but enlarging does not recover detail that the photo lacks. This texture is **not** a final 360° texture.

## Limits of the site render
- The site frames use a transparent background with reflections of a dark-red world. The PET lets the page background show through.
- Condensation was **left out** of this clean version, following the instruction to validate shape and label before any wear.
- No GLB was exported: transmission and volume materials would not be equivalent to the Cycles render.

## Versão publicada no site (06/11/2026)
A pedido do usuário, o site usa `LOOK=antigo` (padrão do `master.py`): **formato novo** (perfil da foto 02) com o **visual da versão 1**.
- Rótulo: `textures/rotulo_v1.png`, a arte antiga, que não reproduz o rótulo real.
- Materiais e luz da versão 1, mais condensação: 1.700 gotas no PET e cerca de 360 no rótulo.
- Para gerar o visual limpo de diagnóstico (rótulo extraído da foto, sem gotas), use `LOOK=limpo`.

Sequências enviadas ao site:
- Giro: 240 quadros (1,5° por quadro). No site ficam `seq/t###` no computador e `seq/tm###` no celular.
- Enchimento: 120 quadros. No site ficam `seq/g###` no computador e `seq/gm###` no celular.
- O player mostra cada quadro inteiro, sem misturar dois quadros.
